# Avoid AI Writing

Detects and fixes AI writing patterns ("AI-isms") that make text sound machine-generated.

## When to Use

- When asked to "remove AI-isms" or "make this sound less like AI"
- After drafting content with AI and before publishing
- When editing text that sounds generated rather than written

## What It Detects

**21 pattern categories:** formatting issues (em dashes, bold overuse, emoji headers, bullet-heavy sections), sentence structure problems (hedging, hollow intensifiers, rule of three), word/phrase replacements (43 entries like leverage->use, utilize->use, robust->reliable), template phrases, transition phrases, significance inflation, copula avoidance, synonym cycling, vague attributions, filler phrases, generic conclusions, chatbot artifacts, promotional language.

## Process

1. **Issues found** — every AI-ism quoted with category
2. **Rewritten version** — plain, direct alternative
3. **What changed** — summary of edits
4. **Second-pass audit** — re-reads the rewrite to catch surviving tells

## Limitations

- Prose only, not code
- Pattern matching is guideline-based, not absolute
- Some flagged words are fine in context
- Cannot verify factual claims
