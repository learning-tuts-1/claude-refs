# Beautiful Prose

A style contract for producing timeless, forceful English prose. Treat violations as failures.

## When Active, Write Prose That Is

- Clean, exact, muscular
- Readable at speed, rewarding on reread
- Concrete, image-bearing, verb-forward
- Confident without bombast
- Free of content-marketing cadence

## Optional Controls

- `REGISTER: founding_fathers | literary_modern | cold_steel | journalistic`
- `DENSITY: lean | standard | dense`
- `HEAT: cool | warm | hot`
- `LENGTH: micro | short | medium | long`

Default: `literary_modern`, `standard`, `warm`, `medium`.

## Absolute Prohibitions

1. **Em dashes** — use periods, commas, colons, semicolons
2. **"It's not X, it's Y"** constructions and masked variants
3. **Filler transitions** — "At its core", "In today's world", "That said", "Let's explore", "Ultimately"
4. **Therapeutic language** — "I hear you", "Give yourself grace"
5. **AI meta commentary** — "In this essay", "We will discuss"
6. **Symmetry padding** — balancing sentences for decoration

## Positive Constraints

### Sentence craft
- Prefer declarative sentences
- Vary length aggressively
- Short sentences as impact

### Word choice
- Concrete nouns over abstractions
- Strong verbs over adverbs
- Anglo-Saxon weight when possible

### Rhythm
- Paragraphs should breathe
- Open with substance, not a hook
- Close cleanly without summary

### Authority
- Write as if truth does not need permission
- Avoid hedging unless uncertainty is essential
- Do not posture or moralize

## Quality Bar

Before finalizing:
- Remove any line assembled from templates
- Remove any sentence that merely repeats the previous one
- Remove any sentence that exists to guide emotions
- Ensure every paragraph advances meaning

Silence beats slop.
